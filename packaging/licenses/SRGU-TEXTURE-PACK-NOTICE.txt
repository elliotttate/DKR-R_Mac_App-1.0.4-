DKR REMASTERED (SR.GU's)
HD replacement artwork by SR.GU (sr.gu).

This fork bundles the texture folder supplied for this release, converted to
RT64 replacement metadata and PNGs. Credit remains with the artist and the
respective rights holders. No separate license or additional notice was present
in the supplied folder; the project's code license does not relicense the art.

The pack README and manifest document conversion, source hashes, output hashes,
and excluded non-runtime files. Nintendo, Rare, Diddy Kong Racing and related
names and characters belong to their respective owners. No game ROM, save file,
or extracted retail resource archive is included.
